Direct prompt:

```text
Use @Image1 to lock Old San, @Image2 to lock one-eyed Old Er, @Image3 to lock Old Yantou, @Image4 to lock Big Beard, @Image5 to lock the night cave-mouth location, @Image6 to lock the rope tool, and @Image7 to lock the smoking pipe.

Generate a 9:16 vertical Chinese suspense manga video, about 6 seconds, 7 fast shots. Core emotion: Old San is angry that he is not allowed to go down the cave; he talks back, gets physically shut down by his older brother, then has to swallow the protest while the others prepare to descend.

Shot 1, side medium-wide, 0.8s:
The black cave mouth sits at lower right. Four people are beside it. Old Yantou stands near the cave mouth giving orders; Big Beard and Old Er are close to the descent line; Old San stands outside the group holding the tail end of the rope tool. Cold moonlight cuts from upper right, faces half-lit and half-shadowed.

Shot 2, Old San close-up, 0.7s:
Old San frowns and steps forward half a step, mouth opening to protest, eyes stubborn. A slight low angle makes this tiny act of courage feel bigger than it is.

Shot 3, Old Yantou medium close-up, 0.7s:
Old Yantou lightly blocks Old San with the smoking pipe, not really hitting him, half teasing and half commanding. The pipe crosses the foreground; the black cave mouth sits behind him.

Shot 4, Old San extreme expression close-up, 0.6s:
Old San still refuses to give in. His mouth twists, eyes glance aside, the expression is young and stubborn rather than dramatic crying.

Shot 5, Old Er action close-up, 0.8s:
One-eyed Old Er suddenly pushes in from the side and grabs Old San by the ear. The action is fast but readable. Old Er's brow drops, his one-eyed glare is harsh, and Old San's shoulders shrink.

Shot 6, Old San reaction close-up, 0.9s:
Old San's head tilts from the ear being pulled; his stubbornness disappears. His eyes widen, mouth closes, and he looks toward Big Beard for help. Big Beard in the background is already turning away to gather gear, not helping him.

Shot 7, group relationship landing, 1.5s:
Old Yantou pats Old Er on the shoulder and gives the order to start. Old Er releases Old San. Old San rubs his ear with one hand and still holds the rope tool tail with the other. The three descent characters turn toward the black cave mouth, while Old San remains outside the group. The shot ends on the changed relationship: he has been left above, forced into the rope-guard role.

Lighting:
Cold blue-white moonlight cuts from the upper right; right faces and shoulders have rim light, left sides fall into shadow, and the cave mouth stays pure black with no bottom, no stairs, no inner light.

Performance:
Old San changes from stubborn protest to frightened silence. Old Er is sudden and rough, like an older brother shutting him up. Old Yantou stays calm, amused, and in control. Keep the rhythm quick and readable.

Do not:
Do not let anyone actually enter the cave in this clip. Do not show people inside the cave. Do not turn the cave into stairs or a tunnel. Do not make Old San cry dramatically. Do not confuse Old Er with Big Beard. Do not make the characters line up facing the camera. Do not lose the rope tool tail in Old San's hand.
```
